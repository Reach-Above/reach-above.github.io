    About this map

    This map identifies 28 school board service areas with an area overlap in the four Williams Treaties (1923) sections from Ontario’s treaty map. It supports education and geographic exploration.

    How to read the map

    Choose a school system, then select a board in the list or on the map. Full board areas shows the whole service area; Overlaps only shows the portion inside the mapped treaty area. Counts and search apply to the selected system.

    Board areas from different school systems cover the same places. When several boards overlap at a map click, choose one from the popup.

    Use the three layer checkboxes independently. Turn off Board polygons to see just schools and the treaty area. School points follow the school system and board-name search, and include schools outside the treaty area. The Full board areas / Overlaps only switch affects polygons only. Click a school point for its name, address, board and location information; schools sharing a point appear together.

    Sources and limitations

    • School boards: four Ontario school board boundary shapefiles supplied for this project, prepared/copied in May 2023. Names and boundaries are retained from those files; that date does not confirm that the boundaries are current. Ontario boundary catalogue.
    • Treaty area: Ontario’s IAO Digital Treaty Map, Treaties layer. All four sections labelled Williams Treaties are displayed with their source coordinates preserved. The service describes its linework as simplified to 5 m in November 2024. Ontario treaty map.
    • Four sections: Ontario pairs Williams Treaties with John Collins’ Purchase, Johnson-Butler Purchase, Rice Lake Purchase and Robinson-Huron Treaty. All four are included; no single section represents the full mapped area.
    • Overlap figures: recalculated from all 72 supplied board boundaries against the combined Ontario sections, using a Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection centred on the region. A small overlap does not mean every school in that board lies in the treaty area.
    • School points: 2,292 located school-category records from the Ontario Ministry of Education contact list dated August 11, 2026, matched to Statistics Canada’s Open Database of Educational Facilities and Natural Resources Canada address estimates. Estimates are labelled in popups. Twenty school records lack reliable coordinates; virtual, summer, continuing-education and treatment/correctional program records are excluded from the map. The full CSV retains these records. Counts represent school records, which can share a building. School data notes and attribution.

    Treaty boundaries are illustrative and estimated. This map does not establish legal boundaries, treaty rights, school attendance eligibility, or a land acknowledgement for any particular location.
