Board inputs and method
All four supplied Ontario school-board shapefiles were reread: 31 English Public, 29 English Catholic, 4 French Public and 8 French Catholic (72 total).
Source board boundaries are NAD83; their coordinates are treated as equivalent to WGS84 at this illustrative map scale.
52 source geometries required make_valid. Non-polygon remnants from repairs were excluded.
Select boards with positive-area intersection, rather than mere boundary contact.
Areas and percentages use Lambert azimuthal equal-area centred at 45N, 79W, WGS84:
+proj=laea +lat_0=45 +lon_0=-79 +datum=WGS84 +units=m +no_defs

Results
28 boards: 11 English Public, 9 English Catholic, 4 French Public and 4 French Catholic.
No additions or removals compared with the former bundle. Overlap areas and percentages have been recomputed; the former metrics must not be reused.
Rainbow DSB and CSP du Grand Nord both intersect the Ontario source. Flags that were specific to uncertainty in the former generalized federal-derived polygon were removed. This confirms mapped intersection only.

Superseded source
The previous bundle used a heavily generalized federal-derived geometry with two-decimal rounding and 0.04-degree line generalization. It does not match the Ontario map footprint. This version replaces that geometry and all dependent overlap outputs.

Limitations
Ontario's map is illustrative; it does not establish actual legal treaty boundaries or rights. The underlying board boundaries and names have not been reconciled to a current board directory. A board-service-area overlap does not establish that every school or school site lies within the mapped treaty area.
