# Schools belonging to the 28 boards overlapping Williams Treaties

Prepared 4 October 2026 for ReachAbove. This is a reviewable compilation, not a
certification that every coordinate is the current school-building location.

## Scope

The roster is the Ontario Ministry of Education's publicly funded school
contact list dated 11 August 2026. Select records by the 28 board IDs that
intersect the corrected Ontario Williams Treaties geometry. Do not select
schools by spatial containment in school-board polygons: English/French and
public/Catholic systems overlap.

All schools in those boards are retained, including schools outside the treaty
area. `inside_williams` indicates point containment in the four Ontario treaty
features. It does not establish legal obligations or treaty rights.

## Files

* `schools_all_records.csv`: every selected Ministry record, including unresolved
  locations and virtual/seasonal/treatment programs. School and board IDs are
  text. Preserve leading zeros when importing into Excel.
* `schools.geojson`: located records in the `school` category, suitable for the
  main map layer. EPSG:4326, longitude then latitude. This includes regular,
  alternative, adult, vocational and junior-high school records, but excludes
  records specifically classified as continuing education, summer, virtual or
  treatment/correctional programs.
* `schools_all_located_records.geojson`: all located physical or program-address
  records. A program at an administrative address is not a separate campus.
* `schools_needing_review.csv`: unresolved nonvirtual locations, address
  conflicts, interpolated points, points outside their board polygon, and points
  within 250 metres of the treaty boundary. A row here can have coordinates.
* `schools_address_changes.csv`: completed/corrected/normalised addresses,
  conflicting addresses, and approved name changes. Original values are retained.
* `board_coverage.csv`: coverage and point-containment counts by board.
* `coverage.json` and `coverage_report.md`: dataset totals and limitations.
* `source/`: original Ontario roster, Statistics Canada data/source list/record
  layout, cached NRCan geolocation responses, and analysis boundary snapshots.
* `verified-addresses.json` and `verified-locations.json`: documented decisions
  and source links used during reconciliation.

Counts are school or program records, not unique buildings. An elementary and
secondary school or several programs can share one address and map coordinate.
The Ministry snapshot can include new campuses before the permanent buildings
are occupied; `site_status_note` records known temporary-placement issues.

## Address reconciliation

Every selected record originally had a Street value, though a few were rural
routes, intersections or otherwise incomplete. Six records were missing a city
and/or postal code. Four were completed using the provider/government directory
or another Ministry school record at the same civic address. The two unresolved
treatment-program records are retained as `ADDRESS_CONFLICT`; verified provider
contact addresses appear separately in `candidate_contact_address`. Those are
not asserted to be the specific program's student location.

S.A. Cawker's Ministry address incorrectly referred to an Oshawa school. Its
current school website confirms 16200 Old Simcoe Road, Port Perry, L9L1P3.
Courtice Secondary's intersection-only address was completed from its board
directory as 1717 Nash Road. Forest View's duplicated Street word was normalised.
Four new DDSB schools were given their approved names from the board's July 2026
announcement, with original roster names preserved. Heartwood's permanent
campus occupancy has not been confirmed; the board described temporary host
placements for the beginning of the 2026 school year.

Contact details beyond address/name were not exhaustively rechecked. In
particular, S.A. Cawker's roster telephone can be wrong along with its original
website. The corrected school website is included; use that site to verify
telephone details before contacting the school.

## Location matching and confidence

1. Match Ontario school ID and board ID to Statistics Canada's ODEF v3.0.1.
   Reuse a point only when the current street still matches after conservative
   bilingual street-type normalization. Keep civic numbers and directions.
2. For unmatched school/program IDs, allow an exact civic address matched to an
   ODEF record in the same municipality or with the same full postal code. If
   source points for that address differ by 100 metres or more, do not accept
   the address match automatically. This is a shared-address match, not an ID
   match, and is labelled medium confidence.
3. For remaining addresses, use the NRCan GeoGratis Geolocation Service. Accept
   only an `INTERPOLATED_POSITION` result with matching civic number, normalized
   street, municipality and province, agreeing candidates within 100 metres,
   and a point within the corresponding board polygon. Reject city centroids,
   street centroids and ambiguous or different-municipality results.
4. NRCan points are road-address estimates, labelled `ADDRESS_INTERPOLATION`.
   They require review before use as precise campus or treaty-boundary locations.
5. One ODEF outlier was corrected: Bayside Secondary's elementary program had a
   longitude over 30 km from its secondary program at the same address. The
   secondary-campus source point was used instead, and the school website
   confirms the shared civic address.
6. Virtual records retain their contact addresses but do not receive student
   campus points. Locations are never assigned to a school using only a board,
   city, postal-code centroid, or a different civic number.

`location_confidence=high` means confidence in the ID/address record match. It
does not mean the coordinates have been independently surveyed or visually
verified. ODEF Ontario records used here have source date 5 July 2024; the
download includes a 17 November 2025 correction note. Address checks catch many
moves, but cannot detect every bad or stale source coordinate. NRCan's underlying
road data vintage is not asserted to be October 2026; that is the lookup date.

`inside_board_boundary` is a diagnostic, not how board membership is assigned.
Small boundary differences or school sites outside board areas can occur.
`near_treaty_boundary` uses a 250 metre review buffer in EPSG:3347; classifications
near boundaries should be checked against actual campus locations.

## Reproducing the delivered files

Install Python 3, Shapely and pyproj. The packaged source snapshots and verified
decisions are sufficient for `python3 build.py`, which recreates `output/`.
`accept_geocoding.py` can re-evaluate the cached geocoder results. The lookup
scripts are one-time extraction tools, not a scheduled refresh service. Do not
treat an older cached response as independent verification of current occupancy.

This compilation is displayed as the school point layer on the ReachAbove map. The map file retains essential display and location provenance fields; the CSV retains the complete records.

## Validation completed

School-ID uniqueness, membership in exactly the 28 selected boards, board totals,
category totals, CSV/GeoJSON coordinate agreement, geographic ranges, required
point provenance, exclusion of virtual/conflicting records, and treaty
point-in-polygon classification were checked. Points near treaty boundaries and
outside board polygons are retained in the review list. Estimates are marked
as ADDRESS_INTERPOLATION and medium confidence. High confidence on ODEF matches
refers to identity/address linkage, not surveyed positional accuracy.

See README.md and per-record notes for source links, dates, methodology and
address/name corrections. The main school-category points are included in the website layer.
